About TAAS Services

The problem

Small business owners know they should adopt cloud tools, data practices and AI, but not what to adopt first, in what order, or whether their team can operate it. They buy tools that go unused, or postpone the decision indefinitely. Research on digital transformation in small businesses identifies limited budget, scarce technical expertise and organisational inertia as the recurring barriers. These are capability problems rather than software problems, so buying more software does not solve them.

Our approach

Consulting delivers the outcome but takes the knowledge away with it, and costs thousands per engagement. Open course catalogues are affordable but require the learner to diagnose their own gap first, which is exactly what these owners cannot do. TAAS Services sits between the two: diagnose the gap, then close it with short practical training that leaves the capability inside the team.

How PathFinder decides

The ranking of dimensions is rules-based, not AI-generated: dimensions are ordered by score, then adjusted for dependency, because data practices built on absent foundations do not hold. Your stated priority promotes its dimension by one position but cannot override a foundational gap. This keeps the recommendation explainable and reproducible, which a black box would not be. The reasoning shown on the learning path page is the actual rule that fired.

Scope of this version

This is the MVP. The assessment, scoring and path generation are complete and functional. Three pilot modules are authored; the remaining modules are in development. AI-written feedback on module exercises (CoachBot) is specified and scheduled for the next build.
